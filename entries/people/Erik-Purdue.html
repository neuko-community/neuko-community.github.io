Erik Purdue ​

OCCUPATION: Director of Operations
ORGANIZATION: St. Juniper Research Campus, Miter Corporation
ACTIVE: Early 2000s (documented)
STATUS: Unknown

Erik Purdue was the Director of Operations at the St. Juniper Research Campus under Miter Corporation oversight. He served during the period when subject G*BOY (G-304) escaped from containment and was subsequently formally admonished by Agent K.

At a glance

  • Director of Operations at St. Juniper Research Campus, Miter Corporation
  • Oversaw facility operations during the G-304 containment period
  • Formally admonished by Agent K following G-304's escape
  • Tenure concluded shortly after the containment breach incident
  • Specific operational decisions leading to breach remain undisclosed

Overview ​

Erik Purdue served as Director of Operations at Miter Corporation's St. Juniper Research Campus, where he held administrative authority over the facility's experimental programs and security protocols. His tenure as director included oversight during the period when subject G-304 was housed at the facility and subsequently escaped from containment.

Career at Miter Corporation ​

Operational Authority ​

As Director of Operations, Purdue held responsibility for:

  • Day-to-day management of St. Juniper Research Campus facilities
  • Security protocols for high-value experimental subjects
  • Coordination between research divisions and Miter Corporation oversight
  • Implementation of directives from higher-level Miter operatives

G-304 Containment ​

Purdue oversaw operations during the period when G-304 (also designated "G*BOY") was contained at the facility. The subject was housed under high-security protocols due to its designation as a Tier-0 experimental subject.

During Purdue's tenure, G-304 escaped from the facility, resulting in a major security breach. The circumstances of the escape and the specific security protocols in place at the time have not been made public.

Formal Admonishment ​

Following G-304's escape, Agent K delivered a formal admonishment to Purdue regarding his handling of facility operations and security protocols during the containment period. The admonishment held Purdue responsible for operational failures that enabled the breach.

The specific nature of the failures cited in the admonishment and the extent of Purdue's culpability remain matters of internal Miter Corporation review.

Career Conclusion ​

Purdue's tenure as Director of Operations concluded following the G-304 containment breach. His current whereabouts and subsequent career activities are unknown.
